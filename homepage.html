ABOUT US

Built for World Robot Olympiad

NextGen Forge is a student robotics team competing in the World Robot Olympiad (WRO). Our project pairs a physical robot — Robo Sanskriti — with a conversational assistant, exploring both sides of how people interact with a robot: an in-person, sensor-triggered greeting experience, and an open-ended question-and-answer interface.

Across both interfaces we focused on the same goals: approachable design, real spoken interaction, and enough flexibility that either piece can run standalone or connect to a live AI backend.
